# Reviewing JewelMind — A Short Introduction

Thank you for agreeing to review JewelMind. This page explains what you'll be looking at and what we're asking of you.

## What JewelMind is

JewelMind is early-stage software that generates 3D jewelry models from a small set of parameters — band width, stone size, number of prongs, and a few others. It's a prototype, not a finished product, and nothing it produces has been reviewed by a jewelry professional before now. We want your honest, practical judgment on it.

## What you'll be asked to do

Look at one or more generated ring designs (usually a solitaire) and tell us, in your own words and from your own experience, what's right, what's wrong, and what's missing. There's no quiz and no score — we want your professional read on the work, the same way you'd assess a rough model from a junior designer or a first CAD pass before real bench work begins.

## How long it takes

However long feels right to you. A quick first look might be 15–20 minutes; a thorough review with the physical/CAD checks described in the forms could take longer. There's no minimum.

## What you'll receive: a Review Package

We'll send you a ZIP file (a "Professional Review Package") containing:

- `README.md` — explains the specific design you're looking at.
- `review-form.md` — a short, ready-to-fill form with our main questions.
- `design.json` — the exact parameters used to generate this design.
- `model.step` and `model.stl` — the actual 3D model, in two common formats. `model.step` is usually the one to open in CAD software; `model.stl` is the mesh format if that's more convenient for you.
- `technical-specification.md` — a plain-text summary of dimensions and settings.
- `forge-report.json`, `geometry-metadata.json`, `component-manifest.json` — technical detail files, useful if you're comfortable with them, entirely skippable if not.
- `manifest.json` — a checksum list, mostly relevant if you're verifying file integrity.

## How to record and return feedback

Fill in `review-form.md` directly, or use one of the more detailed forms in this same folder if it fits what you're reviewing better (there's a stone-setting-specific form, a manufacturing-specific form, and others). Send it back however's easiest — email, a shared doc, marked-up PDF, whatever works for you.

## Honest negative feedback is exactly as valuable as positive feedback

There is no scoring system. "This isn't ready and here's why" is a complete, useful answer. We would rather hear that plainly than have you soften a critique to be polite.
