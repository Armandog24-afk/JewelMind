# Stone v2 Golden Strategy

The suite is now **39 cases**: 23 from Sprints 15–19, plus 16 new
`STV2-*` cases. **Zero existing baselines were updated.**

## How they were created

The sanctioned path, the same one Sprint 19 used:

1. write `design.json` from a real validated `JewelryDefinition`;
2. register the case in `manifest.json`;
3. `generate_candidate_baseline()` — runs the **real** pipeline;
4. `accept_candidate_baseline()` with an explicit reason;
5. remove the transient `candidate.json`;
6. re-run `verify-all`.

Nothing was hand-authored. `INITIAL_BASELINE` for a new case is a creation, not
an update — no accepted baseline was overwritten (QUALITY-GOV-003/004).

## Why pearl and imported stones have no ring Golden

The suite builds **complete rings**. A ring cannot be assembled around a stone
no setting will grip, and both `pearl` and `imported` are `UNSUPPORTED` for
prong and bezel — a sphere has no girdle plane, and imported geometry has no
derived outline.

Faking a ring for them would mean either inventing a setting family or
substituting a different stone. Both are forbidden, and both would make the
Golden protect nothing real.

They are instead covered by:

- **unit tests** — `test_pearl_is_a_real_sphere` (against the analytic sphere
  volume), the full `TestImportedStone` and `TestImportSecurity` classes, and
  `TestMeshTransformRegression`;
- **real import vectors** —
  `specs/stone/v2/test-vectors/import-normalization-vectors.json`, generated by
  exporting a real 6 × 8 × 4mm box, storing it, and importing it back in both
  formats and both units;
- **Geometry Inspection facts** for both.

This is a deliberate, recorded gap rather than an oversight. A stone-only Golden
category would close it and is the obvious future change; it needs a harness
that snapshots a stone without a surrounding ring, which is more than this
sprint's scope.

## A pre-existing finding, recorded rather than avoided

`STV2-001-heart-prong` snapshots `productionIsFullyConnected: false`. The band
sits 0.0681mm from `basket_support` and 0.1112mm from `prongs`, so the head
floats above the band.

**This is not a Stone v2 regression.** It is a function of the stone
**footprint**, not of the heart shape:

| Stone | Connected | band↔basket | band↔prongs |
|---|---|---|---|
| princess 8 × 6 | yes | 0.0000 | 0.0000 |
| princess 8 × 7 | yes | 0.0000 | 0.0000 |
| princess 8 × 7.5 | yes | 0.0000 | 0.0378 |
| princess 8 × 8 | **no** | 0.0681 | 0.1112 |
| **round d = 8.0** | **no** | 0.0681 | 0.1112 |
| heart 8 × 8 | **no** | 0.0681 | 0.1111 |

A `round` stone of diameter 8.0mm — a capability since Sprint 2 — reproduces the
identical distances. The threshold sits near a 7.5mm footprint.

Sprint 20 did not introduce this; its wider shape coverage **surfaced** it. The
baseline records it rather than sidestepping it by choosing a smaller heart,
because a Golden's job is to document real geometry. `verify-all` reports the
case as `PASS_WITH_KNOWN_LIMITATIONS`, and a future change that makes the head
touch the band will show up as a deliberate diff.

Tracked in [`code-mapping-and-gaps.md`](code-mapping-and-gaps.md).

## What each snapshot protects

`GeometrySnapshot` records per component: `componentId`, `role`, `present`,
`solidCount`, `volumeMm3`, `boundingBox`, `topology`, `fallbackUsed` — plus
assembly connectivity and `designConsistency`.

It does **not** record raw component metadata, which is why carrying the full
outline point list in metadata does not enlarge any baseline.

`designConsistency.stoneReferenceIsProductionMetal` remains an exact invariant
across all 39 cases (QUALITY-GOV-013).

## No STEP or STL binaries are committed

Artifacts are validated by export → re-import → compare geometric facts, never
by byte equality: CadQuery's STEP writer embeds variable OpenCascade metadata,
so two exports of identical geometry are not byte-identical
(QUALITY-GOV-007/008).
