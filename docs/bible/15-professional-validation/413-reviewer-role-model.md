# Reviewer Role Model

`ReviewerRole` (`backend/jewelmind/professional_validation/schemas.py`) is a closed `Literal` of 8 values. An attempt to construct a `ReviewerQualification` with any other string is rejected by Pydantic (`backend/tests/test_professional_validation_schemas.py::TestReviewerRole::test_an_invented_role_is_rejected`).

## No single role reviews everything

**One expert is never assumed qualified to validate everything.** A `GEMOLOGIST` reviewing stone-grading claims is not thereby qualified to accept a casting-process assumption, and a `CASTING_SPECIALIST` accepting a manufacturing profile is not thereby qualified to accept a stone-setting claim. `ReviewerQualification.professionalFocus` (see [`414-reviewer-qualification-model.md`](414-reviewer-qualification-model.md)) exists specifically to state the *fit* between one reviewer and one specific review, rather than treating a role as a blanket credential (PROVAL-GOV-004).

The full mapping from role to the review areas it is typically relevant to is maintained separately in [`docs/bible/appendices/professional-reviewer-role-catalog.md`](../appendices/professional-reviewer-role-catalog.md); this document introduces each role with representative examples rather than duplicating that full catalog.

## The 8 `ReviewerRole` values

| Role | Typically has high relevance to |
|---|---|
| `JEWELRY_CAD_DESIGNER` | Model topology, workflow, and editability questions — whether a generated model's structure (feature layout, solid organization) is usable and sensible to a working CAD designer, independent of manufacturing process. |
| `GOLDSMITH_BENCH_JEWELER` | General bench-work feasibility — whether a generated model can actually be worked on at the bench (filing, polishing, finishing) as modeled. |
| `STONE_SETTER` | Prong, seat/bearing, and stone-accessibility questions — whether a stone can actually be set into the generated prong/basket geometry, whether prongs can be pushed over the stone, whether the setter has physical access to do so. |
| `CASTING_SPECIALIST` | Casting-related process assumptions — sprue placement implications, shrinkage/wall-thickness concerns specific to `lost_wax_casting`, cleanup/finishing implications of the as-cast geometry. |
| `RESIN_PRINTING_SPECIALIST` | Resin-printing-specific process assumptions — supports, minimum feature size, and orientation concerns specific to `direct_resin_printing`, distinct from casting concerns. |
| `JEWELRY_MANUFACTURING_ENGINEER` | Cross-process manufacturing feasibility not specific to one method — tolerance stack-ups, general producibility questions spanning casting and printing. |
| `GEMOLOGIST` | Stone-specific claims — dimensional/proportion assumptions about a stone shape, general stone-handling implications, independent of the metal setting around it. |
| `CAD_INTEROPERABILITY_SPECIALIST` | Export-workflow and CAD-interoperability claims — whether a JewelMind STEP/STL export actually imports correctly, and how editable it is, in real external CAD software (Rhino, MatrixGold, and similar). |

## A concrete illustration from the real test vectors

`specs/professional-validation/v1/test-vectors/qualification-vectors.json` contains three real, generated `ReviewerQualification` → `typicallyRelevantReviewAreas` pairs — worth quoting directly here because they show the shape of the fit-for-review mapping concretely rather than abstractly:

- `role: "STONE_SETTER"`, `professionalFocus: "prong and bezel settings"` → `["setting", "prongs", "seat/bearing", "stone accessibility"]`.
- `role: "CASTING_SPECIALIST"`, `professionalFocus: "lost-wax casting production"`, `processes: ["lost_wax_casting"]` → `["manufacturing method: lost_wax_casting", "sprue/cleanup implications"]`.
- `role: "CAD_INTEROPERABILITY_SPECIALIST"`, `professionalFocus: "STEP import verification across CAD packages"`, `softwareExperience: ["Rhino", "MatrixGold"]` → `["export workflow", "STEP/STL import fidelity"]`.

None of these three vectors is a real reviewer or a real review — they are illustrative fixtures, generated by constructing the real `ReviewerQualification` Pydantic model and are validated as part of `backend/tests/test_professional_validation_specs.py`. They show the *shape* of the role-to-relevance relationship this framework expects, not an actual completed qualification.

## Roles are not exclusive and not exhaustive by design

A single real person can plausibly hold more than one of these roles' relevant experience (e.g. a bench jeweler who also does casting). This model does not force one-role-per-person; `ReviewerQualification` (see [`414-reviewer-qualification-model.md`](414-reviewer-qualification-model.md)) is one record per reviewer *per role claimed*, so the same person reviewing under two different capacities would carry two distinct qualification records with distinct `professionalFocus` statements. A new role beyond these 8 requires an RFC per [`410-validation-governance.md`](410-validation-governance.md)'s "When an RFC is required" section.
